# Experiment 042 — registry-class plus explicit two-adult property quotes

## Hypothesis

The strict failure in Experiment 032 was partly caused by requiring one search snippet to prove both star class
and occupancy. For cities with a frozen official-register class manifest, a one-city Luna call can join exact
property identity/class evidence to separate public Booking two-adult quotes and produce independent one-star
ground-truth observations.

## Protocol

- One independent GPT-5.6 Luna-class context per city; one city only.
- Three cities with frozen official one-star manifests: Barcelona, Lisbon, and Da Nang.
- Three listed properties per city; exactly one ordered search per property.
- No page reads, retries, fallback sources, arithmetic, FX conversion, averaging, or cross-city evidence.
- Register class evidence and price/occupancy evidence remain separate provenance fields.

## Pre-registered verdict rules

- Promote the join contract to a broader ground-truth panel if at least 2/3 cities have at least two valid
  property quotes and no identity/class reconciliation error.
- Any accepted quote is an independent property observation, not a city average. A future basket statistic still
  requires a frozen selection rule, at least 30 cities, and 10 locked holdouts.
- Reject any result that relies on an inferred property identity, inferred occupancy, or a from/lowest price.
