# Experiment 042 — verdict

**Verdict: reject the registry-join route as a sufficiently productive one-star panel; retain one guarded
property candidate for future ground-truth work.**

Three independent one-city Luna calls used frozen official-register manifests and exactly one property search per
listed property. All nine searches were compliant with no reads, retries, fallback sources, arithmetic, FX
conversion, averaging, or cross-city evidence.

Only one of nine properties produced a strict quote: Hotel LX ROSSIO in Lisbon, €63/night for two guests with
taxes included. Barcelona's Coronado quote had unknown tax treatment; all Da Nang properties failed price,
occupancy, or identity reconciliation. The Lisbon quote also needs an identity review because the search result
address differs from the frozen register address.

The pre-registered threshold (at least two cities with at least two valid quotes) failed. Do not compute a basket,
map the quote to `accom_1_star`, or fit a correction. Preserve the official class evidence and rejected reasons;
future ground-truth collection needs a source with stable dates, tax treatment, and exact identity.
