# Verdict - Experiment 071

**Reject the per-person activity screen at the feasibility gate.**

- 12/12 one-city Luna contexts were protocol-compliant.
- Strict coverage was budget 3/12, mid-range 0/12, and high-end 1/12.
- No city had all three activity inputs.

The four accepted facts are retained as one-person/per-person source evidence. The deferred factor of two was never
applied, and no activity product value, tier model, or accuracy claim is authorized. A future activity method needs a
materially different source/contract and independent two-adult validation.
