# Verdict — reject broad accommodation panel collection

The candidate is rejected as a general route to accommodation model ground truth.

Only **4 of 60 cells (6.7%)** survived the frozen compatibility rules. Three of ten cities had any fact, no city had all six classes, and the 2-, 3-, and 4-star classes had zero coverage. The surviving evidence was Prague hostel private (1,030 CZK), Nairobi dorm/private (€12/€13), and Don Det 1-star (CNY 75).

The dominant failures were structural rather than mere lack of search effort: result pages expose `from` prices or ranges; bundle multiple nights or beds; omit occupancy or formal class; show stale/promotional prices; or require arithmetic. Relaxing those rules would change the estimand and manufacture apparent coverage.

This pilot therefore does not justify a 30-city complete-case panel using one broad prompt. The next accommodation experiment should test a narrow, date-fixed source/query contract that yields comparable class observations, or reconsider whether formal 1–4-star classes can be validated from a separate manually curated benchmark. The four facts here are feasibility evidence only and must not be used as fitted coefficients.

Telemetry is incomplete by design of the delegated execution surface: searches and page reads were counted, but exact provider model ID, parameters, tokens, latency, and cost were not exposed and remain null.
