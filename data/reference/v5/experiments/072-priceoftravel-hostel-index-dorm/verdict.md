**Promote the Price of Travel Hostel Index only to scaling and independent validation.**

The candidate is not yet a production anchor: the reference period is stale, the statistic is a selected named-hostel
observation rather than a current city median, and no two-bed accuracy comparison has been run. Deterministic code

authorized until the 30-city/10-locked-holdout validation gates pass.
