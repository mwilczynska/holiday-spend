# Experiment 055 verdict - reject Skyscanner class panel

## Decision

Reject the Skyscanner class-average route under the strict contract. Do not map any class, treat unknown-tax
snippets as observed inputs, or fit a ratio from the returned 3-/4-star candidates.

## Evidence

- Twelve independent single-city Luna-class contexts issued exactly four ordered searches each (1-, 2-, 3-,
  and 4-star Skyscanner pages; 48 searches total).
- No page reads, retries, arithmetic, FX conversion, averaging, fallback sources, or cross-city evidence.
- Strict coverage was **0/48**: 1-star 0/12, 2-star 0/12, 3-star 0/12, and 4-star 0/12; no city was complete.
- Several 3-/4-star snippets had exact city/class, a two-adult one-room selector, and a numeric average, but tax
  treatment was absent or the currency/class evidence was malformed. 1-/2-star rows were absent or ambiguous,
  not positively established as class absence.

The source may be useful for a separately amended tax/price-statistic experiment, but this panel does not provide
definition-compatible observations for production or model fitting.
