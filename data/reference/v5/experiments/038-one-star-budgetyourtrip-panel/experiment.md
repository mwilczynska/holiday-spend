# Experiment 038 — one-star BudgetYourTrip broad panel

Date: 2026-07-31

## Hypothesis

The guarded BudgetYourTrip one-star candidate from Experiment 034 will provide a broad exact-city class statistic
with visible sample counts often enough to support a later occupancy/calibration study. Zero-denominator and tiny
sample rows must fail closed.

## Pre-registered panel

Twenty new cities are tested independently: Kuala Lumpur, Singapore, Ho Chi Minh City, Da Nang, Manila, Jakarta,
Seoul, Taipei, Buenos Aires, Rio de Janeiro, Santiago, Lima, New Delhi, Mumbai, Istanbul, Athens, Rome, London,
Paris, and Vienna. Each call issues exactly one BudgetYourTrip-restricted search; no reads, retries, fallback,
arithmetic, FX, or cross-city evidence.

## Acceptance

Count strict class/date/source rows, explicit occupancy, visible sample size, zero-denominator exclusions, and
protocol telemetry. This tranche extends source feasibility only; no one-star product value, correction, or ratio
is fitted. A later model still needs definition-matched 30-city/10-holdout accuracy.
