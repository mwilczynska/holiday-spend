# Experiment 008 — omitted-anchor ground-truth feasibility

**Date:** 2026-07-31  
**Status:** completed bounded pilot; not 30-city/10-holdout acceptance evidence

## Hypothesis

One delegated target-class research task can collect enough definition-compatible facts for the nine
anchors omitted by Experiment 007 to make a broad multi-category ground-truth panel practical.

## Design

Ten stratified cities were fixed: Lisbon, Copenhagen, Hanoi, Prague, Bangkok, Barcelona, San Francisco,
Da Nang, Nairobi, and Don Det. The task searched only street food, premium meal, 1/2/4-star rooms,
cocktail, wine glass, half-day group activity, and full-day premium activity. It performed no arithmetic,
FX, tiers, or estimates. Ranges and basis-incompatible packages were retained as failure notes rather than
converted to point values.

The bounded task used three web-search calls containing ten city queries and one page-read call containing
six URLs. Four pages opened and two returned cache misses. Orchestration did not expose exact model ID,
parameters, tokens, latency, or cost.
