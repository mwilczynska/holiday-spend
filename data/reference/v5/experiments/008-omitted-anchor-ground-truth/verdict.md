# Verdict — reject broad direct collection as the ground-truth method

Only **4 of 90 cells (4.4%)** met the frozen basis:

- Nairobi street-food item: 1;
- San Francisco cocktail: 1;
- Bangkok and Barcelona half-day group activities: 2.

No compatible 1-star, 2-star, or 4-star two-adult room rate was collected. Premium two-person meals,
wine glasses, and full-day premium group activities also had zero coverage.

The low result is mainly a definition problem, not a search outage. Results commonly exposed ranges,
private vehicle/package prices, per-person tasting menus, stale promotions, hotel guidance without class
and occupancy, or venue names without item prices. Counting these would manufacture compatibility.

Therefore a single broad web task is rejected as the way to build fitting ground truth for the omitted
anchors. Future evidence collection should use narrowly specified source panels per relationship, and the
product estimands should be reconsidered where stable city-level observations are inherently unavailable.
This pilot says nothing about model accuracy and cannot substitute for the required 30-city sample or
locked holdout.
