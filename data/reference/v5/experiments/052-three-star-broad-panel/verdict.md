# Experiment 052 verdict — reject three-star property route

## Decision

Reject the broad three-star property route under the strict contract. Do not map it to `accom_3_star`, fit a
correction, or infer one-room occupancy from a “2 adults” selector.

## Evidence

- Twelve independent single-city Luna-class contexts: Amsterdam, Prague, Copenhagen, Dublin, Vienna, Budapest,
  Istanbul, Seoul, Sydney, Vancouver, Nairobi, and Buenos Aires.
- Exactly three ordered searches per city (36 total): Google Hotels, Expedia, Booking.com.
- No page reads, retries, arithmetic, FX conversion, averaging, or cross-city evidence.
- Strict accepted quotes: **0/12**, below the 6/12 promotion gate.
- Most candidates had exact class, price, and tax but omitted explicit one-room occupancy; others were from prices,
  generic averages, wrong class, nearby cities, or missing class.

The evidence indicates occupancy proof—not price or star-class discovery—is the dominant blocker. Any protocol
relaxation would be a pre-registered estimand change and would require independent accuracy validation; no silent
substitution is allowed.
