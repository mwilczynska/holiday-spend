# Experiment 043 — verdict

**Verdict: reject Google Hotels as a sufficiently productive one-star property-panel route; retain Cape Town as
a single ground-truth candidate.**

Six independent one-city Luna calls issued exactly three Google-Hotels-restricted searches each. All 18 searches
were compliant with no reads, retries, fallback sources, arithmetic, FX conversion, averaging, or cross-city
evidence. Only Cape Town produced a strict quote: Road Lodge Cape Town International Airport, USD 63 all-in for
one night, exact one-star class, and explicit two-adult occupancy. Bangkok, Barcelona, Hanoi, Lisbon, and New York
City failed on class, identity, occupancy, or tax/price evidence.

The pre-registered 3/6 promotion threshold failed. Do not map the Cape Town property quote to `accom_1_star`,
fit a correction, or treat Google Hotels coverage as complete. Keep the row as independent ground-truth evidence
only; the one-star route remains unresolved.
