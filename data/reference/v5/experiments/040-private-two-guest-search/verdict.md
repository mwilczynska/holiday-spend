# Experiment 040 — verdict

**Verdict: promote explicit two-guest snippets to a property-panel/ground-truth candidate; reject them as a
city-wide private-hostel anchor.**

Six independent one-city Luna calls issued exactly two ordered searches each (Hostelworld, then Booking.com).
All 12 search operations were compliant with no reads, retries, fallback sources, arithmetic, FX conversion, or
cross-city evidence. Three cities passed the strict explicit-occupancy contract: London, Lisbon, and Hanoi. Each
is a dated named-property quote for two adults/max two, not a city average; taxes/fees and source currency are
preserved. Melbourne, New York City, and Tokyo failed closed on from-prices, ambiguous class, or missing quotes.

The pre-registered 3/6 source-feasibility threshold is met, so expand this route only as a frozen property-panel
collection method for independent ground truth. Do not use the three rows as direct `accom_hostel_private_room`
city costs, fit a city-wide correction from them, or treat a dated property quote as an average. A broader
city-stratified panel and aggregation rule must pass the 30-city/10-holdout accuracy gates first.
