# Experiment 036 — activity Budget Your Trip panel

Date: 2026-07-31

## Hypothesis

The BudgetYourTrip activity contract promoted in Experiment 035 will retain complete, explicitly one-person/day
activity and budget/mid/luxury entertainment coverage across cost bands and regions, including locked cities.

## Pre-registered panel

Development cities: Bangkok, Prague, Mexico City, Tokyo, Cape Town, Nairobi, San Francisco. Locked holdouts:
Helsinki, New York City, Sydney. Each city is a separate Luna-class invocation with exactly two restricted
searches. No reads, retries, fallback sources, arithmetic, scaling, FX conversion, averaging, or cross-city
evidence.

## Acceptance

Count strict rows, complete cities, party basis, date/source identity, and quality warnings. `activities_free = 0`
remains definitional. This panel does not fit or map product values; the later gate requires independent
definition-matched accuracy for two-person scaling and tier semantics.
