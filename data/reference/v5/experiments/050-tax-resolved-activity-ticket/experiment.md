# Experiment 050 — tax-resolved official activity ticket

## Hypothesis

A query that explicitly requests tax-resolved official admission prices will turn the two otherwise-compatible
official budget-ticket candidates from Experiment 046 into a production-feasible direct activity input.

## Protocol

- One independent GPT-5.6 Luna-class context per city; one city only.
- Six cities: Lisbon, Hanoi, Bangkok, Cape Town, Barcelona, and New York City.
- Exactly one ordered search per call.
- No page reads, retries, fallback sources, arithmetic, FX conversion, or cross-city evidence.

## Pre-registered verdict rules

- Promote the tax-resolved budget-ticket source pattern if at least 4/6 cities pass the strict contract.
- Accepted rows remain one-person source inputs. No two-person scaling or product mapping follows this feasibility
  test without the 30-city/10-holdout validation gate.

## Results

The six-city panel produced **2/6 strict tax-resolved tickets**: SeaLife Bangkok and Oceanário de Lisboa. Each city
used exactly one ordered search (six total), with no reads, retries, fallback sources, arithmetic, FX conversion, or
cross-city evidence. Four cities failed on unknown tax, from/starting prices, absent numeric adult price, or bundles.

**Verdict:** reject the 4/6 promotion gate. Retain Bangkok and Lisbon as direct source candidates only; do not map
the route to `activities_budget` without a broader validation panel.
