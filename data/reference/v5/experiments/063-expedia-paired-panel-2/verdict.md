## Decision

Reject the pre-registered paired-panel gate. Do not map Expedia rows or fit class ratios. Retain the strict rows for
the pooled source ceiling audit, but stop treating additional identical tranches as evidence of a production-complete
route until the pooled complete-case and model-eligibility counts are measured.

- Twelve entirely new single-city GPT-5.6 Luna-class contexts issued exactly three ordered Expedia searches each

The fresh tranche confirms severe class-dependent sparsity, especially for 2-star. No product mapping or modelling
follows. Next work is a pooled evidence/ceiling audit across all Expedia tranches and prior direct observations.
