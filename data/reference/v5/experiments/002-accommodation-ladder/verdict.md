# Experiment 002 Verdict — Accommodation Candidate Rejected

The retained hotel ladder is directionally useful but not final v5 evidence. Its class relations have only
16 cities, below the pre-registered 30-city requirement, while the blended-hostel relation has 13 and fails
to identify dorm beds versus private rooms. The only full-inventory first-page check has a 3.945× sliding
window spread, and headline/list ratios vary from 0.451× to 1.834× across the captured cities.

**Verdict:** retain the ratio evidence as a candidate baseline; reject it as the final accommodation method.
The next accommodation experiment must collect definition-matched ground truth across at least 30 cities
and test an occupancy-labelled source.
