# Verdict — partially promote the Booking 3/4-star templates

The direct-template method found **10 of 30** facts (33.3%): both 3- and 4-star city averages for every city. The Booking pages explicitly stated exact city/class USD averages per night, and defaulted to two adults and one room.

Do not promote the remainder. Booking 1-star URLs failed; Booking/Trip 2-star pages did not yield an accepted average and showed prohibited `from` prices; Hostelworld supplied only `Dorms From` and `Privates From` values, so no median was admissible.

The next test should audit the 3/4-star averages for stability and definition compatibility on a broader held-out city panel. Lower hotel classes and hostels still need a different source contract.
