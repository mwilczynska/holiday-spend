# Experiment 011 — direct class-page templates

Date: 2026-07-31

The exact prompt was `docs/prompts/llm_prompt_city_cost_v5_experiment_011.md`. One GPT-5.6 Luna-class delegated task opened the constructed Booking.com and Hostelworld URLs before fallback Trip.com templates. Five cities and six accommodation measures were tested. This is source feasibility only.

Found values require page-stated city, exact class, USD currency, and a per-night city average. Hostel medians require at least five non-`from` dorm or private prices; hotel property medians require at least three compatible non-`from` prices. No ratios, FX, inferred stars, or unsupported class absence were used.
