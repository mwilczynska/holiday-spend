# Experiment 046 verdict — reject official activity-page route as a complete source

## Decision

Reject the tested official-page route as a production-complete activity source. Do not map any row or fit an
activity model from this feasibility panel.

## Evidence

- Six independent single-city Luna-class contexts: Lisbon, Hanoi, Bangkok, Cape Town, Barcelona, and New York City.
- Exactly three ordered official-source searches per city: 18 searches total.
- No page reads, retries, marketplace fallbacks, arithmetic, FX conversion, averaging, or cross-city evidence.
- Strict compatible cells: **0/18** (budget 0/6, mid-range 0/6, high-end 0/6), below the preregistered 3/6 gate.
- Two budget tickets (Bangkok and Cape Town) were definition-compatible apart from unknown tax treatment; they are
  retained as rejected evidence, not observed product inputs.
- No mid-range or high-end cell supplied all required duration, party, price, premium, and tax fields.

Official pages may remain a future budget-ticket evidence route if tax treatment can be resolved without a second
production call. This experiment does not establish that route, and it supplies no complete activity methodology.
