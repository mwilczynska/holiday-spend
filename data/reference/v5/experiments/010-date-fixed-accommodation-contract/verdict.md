# Verdict — reject search-index date-fixed contract

The strict contract produced **0 of 30** admissible facts. Search retrieval did not preserve the requested 15–16 September 2026 stay. Hostelworld exposed undated `from` rates; Booking.com and Hotels.com results exposed other dates, multi-night totals, or promotions.

This rejects the assumption that adding fixed dates to broad web-search queries makes indexed accommodation snippets definition-compatible. It does not establish that interactive provider pages are unusable, only that the target-class search path tested here cannot reliably carry the fixed-date contract into retrieved evidence.
