candidate and pivot to a targeted 2-/3-star missing-class panel.** All 24/24 independent one-city Luna contexts
were protocol-compliant and produced 32 strict-or-proxy rows under the exact-host guard. The pooled audit has 99

The pre-registered gate fails for both relationships: 2-star←3-star remains at 20 and 4-star←3-star is one city
short at 29. Generic all-city trends, district pages, wrong-city pages, and sparse 2-star results dominate the
2-star gap. No coefficient, class split, or product mapping is authorized. Retain the 32 rows with explicit
`source_locale_proxy`/imputed-currency provenance. The next experiment may target the missing 2-/3-star pair
boundary, but must remain a new pre-registered source-coverage screen and still requires independent
explicit-two-adult accuracy validation before production use.
