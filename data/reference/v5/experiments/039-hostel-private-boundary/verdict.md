# Experiment 039 — verdict

**Verdict: retain the dorm-bed source boundary; reject the private-room route as a production anchor.**

Six independent one-city Luna calls issued exactly two targeted searches each. All 12 searches were
protocol-compliant with no reads, retries, fallback sources, arithmetic, FX conversion, or cross-city evidence.

The dorm measure was found in all six cities as an exact city-level central one-adult-bed statistic. This is a
usable observed input only after deterministic two-traveller scaling and currency conversion; neither operation
was performed by the extractor.

The private-room measure was found in **0/6** cities under the strict contract. Each source exposed a private-room
number, but none explicitly established two-adult/two-guest occupancy. The values therefore remain unknown-basis
source evidence and cannot be promoted, ratio-fitted, or silently mapped to `accom_hostel_private_room`.

Keep the Hostelz/BudgetYourTrip route as a guarded dorm input candidate with page/filter provenance. The private
hostel gap remains open and requires either definition-matched observations or a separately validated model.
