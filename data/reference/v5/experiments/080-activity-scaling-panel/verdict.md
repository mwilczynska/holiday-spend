# Experiment 080 verdict

**Promote deterministic scaling to definition validation; do not accept product mapping.** The panel had 30/30
protocol-compliant calls and 28/30 complete exact-city per-person/day BudgetYourTrip tier sets, passing the registered
source screen. Fukuoka was `not_found`; Rome's multi-city itinerary rows were rejected. Holdout completeness was 8/10,
below the final 95% gate. The only transformation is the deterministic factor of two already encoded in the v5
derivation contract. Independent ground truth is still required to establish that reported-spend entertainment tiers
are suitable proxies for the three activity estimands.
