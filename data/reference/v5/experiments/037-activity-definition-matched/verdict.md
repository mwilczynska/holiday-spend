# Experiment 037 — verdict

**Verdict: reject BudgetYourTrip as a definition-matched activity-anchor source.**

Lisbon, Hanoi, and Copenhagen each received one compliant one-city call with exactly three restricted searches.
Across nine searches there were no reads, retries, fallback sources, arithmetic, FX conversion, averaging, or
cross-city evidence.

Zero of nine rows passed the frozen activity definitions. Copenhagen supplied a single attraction ticket but not
an adult/two-person basis. Lisbon and Copenhagen supplied full-day private tours with unspecified group size;
Hanoi supplied no duration-and-party-compatible row. No half-day group row passed in any city. These candidates
cannot be scaled or mapped to `activities_budget`, `activities_mid_range`, or `activities_high_end`.

The generic BudgetYourTrip entertainment contract remains a source candidate for a separately defined product
interpretation, but it does not satisfy the current data dictionary. Preserve the rejection and do not weaken the
party/duration gates after seeing the 10-city generic panel succeed.
