# Experiment 054 verdict - reject model fitting for current evidence

The accepted direct ledger does not support fitting any pre-registered material relationship. The largest
accommodation class coverage is one direct 4-star city and there are zero directly observed 1-star, 2-star, or
3-star rows in the canonical ledger, so every hotel-class relation has zero matched cities. Private-hostel versus
dorm has zero matched cities. The activity relations have only one matched city each (Vancouver) because the
definition-compatible half-day and full-day observations are sparse.

All six relationships are below the required 30 matched cities and 10 locked holdouts. No coefficient, modelled
value, correction, or product mapping was created. The food/drink primitive rows and paid-attraction rows remain
useful direct inputs for deterministic baskets, but this audit does not claim they validate accommodation or
activity tier models.

**Decision:** reject model fitting for this evidence boundary. Collect definition-compatible paired observations
or test a materially different source/estimand before attempting a model.
