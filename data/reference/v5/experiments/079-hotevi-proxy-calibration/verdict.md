# Experiment 079 verdict

**Reject proxy calibration.** All 18/18 new calls were protocol-compliant and complete, but only 19 cities matched
an explicit Expedia 3-star row and 15 matched an explicit Expedia 4-star row. Locked holdout matches were 8 and 5,
below the mandatory 30-city/10-holdout gates. The unfitted proxy screen was unstable and inaccurate (overall median
APE 17.65% / p90 109.68% for 3-star; 35.92% / 183.02% for 4-star; holdout median APE 23.46% / 197.30% and
56.46% / 431.91%). Retain HOTEVI only as labelled research evidence; do not split groups, fit coefficients, or map
product tiers.
