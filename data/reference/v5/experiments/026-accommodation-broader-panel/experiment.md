# Experiment 026 — broader accommodation panel with promoted bed boundary

Date: 2026-07-31

## Hypothesis

The Experiment 025 collection boundary (explicit one-bed dorm inputs plus strict two-adult hotel/private-room
identity) may produce enough compatible rows across a more varied city sample to make a 30-city/10-holdout
panel practical.

## Production-shaped test

Each Luna-class invocation researches exactly one city with the unchanged versioned Experiment 025 prompt and
exactly six searches. This tranche tests Lisbon (Portugal), Hanoi (Vietnam), and Copenhagen (Denmark), one
independent invocation per city. No direct page reads, retries, fallback sources, arithmetic, FX conversion,
or cross-city evidence are allowed.

## Acceptance

Use the deterministic Experiment 025 audit rules. A dorm observation is one explicit non-`from` bed/night
input; private hostel and all hotel classes require explicit two-adult per-room identity. This tranche measures
retrieval feasibility only. Do not fit a model or call the panel complete unless 30 cities are complete and at
least 10 complete cities are reserved in the locked holdout.
