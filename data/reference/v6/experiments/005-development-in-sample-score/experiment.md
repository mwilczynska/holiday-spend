# Experiment 005 — development in-sample product-tier score

**Status:** deterministic development-panel score; never a holdout result.

This experiment scores only pairs formed from the unsealed 25-city development ledger, the independent
BudgetYourTrip food panel, official-attraction rows for the budget activity tier, and the Stage-B production
prediction bundles from experiment 006. It never reads `holdout-seal.json`, either holdout ledger, or any
`revealed_once` measure.

The score intentionally reports three classes:

- evaluable in-sample tiers with a production prediction and independent truth;
- `activities_free`, which is definitional and not empirically scored; and
- blocked tiers where independent full-basket truth does not exist (drinks) or the only available truth is
  the production source itself (BudgetYourTrip mid/high activities). Street food has no direct daily-tier
  truth and is checked only through the food basket.

`results.json` is regenerated by `scripts/score-v6-development-panel-in-sample.mjs` after the prediction
bundle is generated. Its `inSample: true` and `holdout: false` fields are mandatory.
