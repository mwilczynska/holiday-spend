## How many cities a comparison needs

`sample-size.mjs` (output in `sample-size-2026-11-19.txt`) uses the sweep lanes and Luna's 333 dev answers.

- Per-city paired differences against Luna (both with own per-anchor fits) have a standard deviation of about 0.065
  for the OpenAI models and about 0.11 for the Claude models. Cities needed for 80% power, two-sided 5%:
  difference 0.015: 140 to 170 (OpenAI), 280 to 740 (Claude); 0.03: 35 to 45, 70 to 185; 0.05: 13 to 16, 26 to 67.
- A per-anchor correction is nearly at its best from 40 cities (held-out 0.193 against 0.191 on all 333). The
  production country-in-region table needs many more: 0.189 at 40, 0.176 at 80, 0.171 at 120, 0.163 at 333.
- On 20 cities, Luna's country offsets plus a model's own per-anchor shift looked no better than the model's own
  per-anchor fit (Sonnet xhigh 0.203 against 0.176). The 80-city lanes below reverse this: with 80 cities the shift is
  estimated well enough that the transfer beats the per-anchor fit for every Sonnet and Opus setting. Superseded.

Consequence: compare candidates on per-anchor fits at 60 to 120 cities (the hash-ordered `--sample` lists nest, so the
existing 20 are reused), and collect the full 333 only for a model that will get its own production table.

Findings:

  equal. Sonnet max is the only Claude setting level with Luna (0.178 against 0.176) and the most accurate Claude
