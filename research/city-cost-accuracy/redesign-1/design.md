# Redesign 1: listed-cappuccino measurement screen

Status: **designer_draft_requires_coordinator_validation**. Coordinator-selected GPT-6 Astra/Medium, Codex subagent, designcall5. The companion JSON is the complete normative contract: exact prompt templates, schemas, schedule, calculations, gates, budgets, fixtures and input hashes.

This is a **new evidence stratum**, not a rescue of restart-1 or a candidate improvement. Run one measurement experiment, assess it, and stop. No estimates, FX retrieval, candidate tuning or production changes are allocated.

The target is the geometric mean menu-listed price of one regular hot cappuccino across four fixed search-result venue slots in each of Hanoi Hoan Kiem, Oaxaca de Juarez Centro and Copenhagen Indre By. These released development cities span regional/language contexts. The frame is search-visible cafes in these districts, not a random city sample. One named cup is the unit; volume and service-mode uncertainty remain visible. Unknown charges are recorded separately, never guessed; listed prices cannot validate the app payable-price estimate.

Three collectors each make one exact search, freeze four distinct-brand/domain slots before opening them, then open each once. No replacement slots. Three fresh blind verifiers receive only URLs and identities and independently re-extract each item. This is independent extraction of the same public source, not independent source truth. The maximum is **6 reference workers and 27 actions**, plus this design, at most one prospective repair, and one final assessment. Each route has at most 5 or 4 actions, each worker 600 seconds from actual spawn, concurrency at most 3. All acquisition finishes within 48 hours; each verifier starts within 24 hours of its collector.

There are 12 fixed slots and 24 expected extractions. Missing/disputed rows cannot disappear from the denominator. Every city needs four matched eligible distinct-menu slots. The two raw amounts remain immutable; their geometric midpoint is only an analytical centre. Unit/venue identity disagreements are missing pairs, never semantic coordinator repairs. Hashing stays in coordinator sidecars; schemas and a null-price fixture are delivered before tools, and the source frame is saved before opens.

For each city, x_i is the mean of collector and verifier log amounts, and mu is its four-slot mean. R is the largest paired log-price discrepancy. Enumerate all six two-slot subsets; S is the largest distance of a subset mean from mu. All three disjoint 2-versus-2 partitions have maximum contrast 2S. Also report leave-one-out sensitivity and range. U=S+R is an empirical sensitivity envelope, **not a confidence bound**; it cannot bound population bias, absent venues or shared source mistakes.

The useful improvement is an absolute **0.10 mean absolute-log-loss reduction**, not the old 10% relative-loss criterion. A reference-log movement up to U shifts one fixed predictor loss by at most U, and a paired method-loss difference by at most 2U. Require every U<=0.025, macro 2*mean(U)<=0.05, and every R<=ln(1.01), spending no more than half the useful margin on observed measurement sensitivity. A future central gain would need 0.10+2*mean(U) to retain 0.10 throughout this empirical envelope. No predictors run here; no gain or estimator repeat variation is measured.

Complete data above a threshold yield **fail_precision**. Missing paired coverage yields **inconclusive_missing_evidence**, with operational feasibility failed. Protocol violations yield **invalid_protocol**. Only complete coverage and all thresholds passing yield **pass_listed_price_precision_only**. A pass is an exploratory measurement result and does not authorize a later comparison or tuning campaign; it does not establish app, payable-price, population or actual-spend accuracy.

Worst-case cumulative consumption: 19 estimates, 100 reference actions, 19 reference workers, 44 estimate-retrieval accounting actions and 7 design calls. Preserve the 100-estimate / 80-reference-action / 16-reference-worker confirmation reserve. Transfer only finite non-confirmation allocations via a linked journal; closed artifacts remain unchanged. Historical unknown actions remain unknown beyond their accounting charge.

Before sources, validate schemas, granular parser, mathematical boundary fixtures, ledger and raw-byte hashes; freeze expanded prompts and harness. Scientific ambiguity requires the single prospective designer repair, never coordinator-invented thresholds. Final assessment and report end this experiment.
