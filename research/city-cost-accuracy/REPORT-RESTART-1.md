# Designer-led research closeout: insufficient evidence

Completed 14 October 2026 UTC (13 October local). This closes the revised goal at its frozen failed-feasibility
gate. The report is an evidence-limited research outcome, not a successful accuracy improvement or validation
of the full city budget. Retain production v1.1; no candidate was tested or selected.

This report supersedes the activation/status wording in historical `REPORT.md`. Its "replacement inactive"
banner describes the earlier owner stop, before the explicit restart; that immutable baseline document is
retained unchanged for provenance. The restarted bounded run is now closed under its own feasibility rule.
The saved goal prompt also retains its executed activation text; `PLAN.md` records the current terminal status.

## What the program established

The earlier unchanged-v1.1 baseline measured agreement with sparse named offers in four cities: successful-only
median absolute percentage error **18.9%**, p90 **91.7%**, and failure-penalized mean absolute log loss
**0.3979154954**. There were 15 distinct city/measure references, 45 expected repeat cells and three failed
cells. These observations do not measure city-wide price distributions, complete daily spending or matched
dated hotel accuracy. The old baseline and its original stop report remain immutable historical evidence.

The revised program attempted a stronger reference design before testing improvements. Its executed pipeline
produced **zero usable primary meal aggregates and zero qualifying cities**, against a requirement of at least
three independent usable venues in at least four of the six original cities. None of three hotel probes obtained
an exact-date, class/occupancy/mandatory-charge-matched offer. This execution cannot support the proposed
meal-prompt comparison, broader development expansion or confirmation.

GPT-6 Astra/Medium's [mandatory assessment](restart-1-prep/designer-assessment.md) applies the original gate
and recommends a bounded insufficient-evidence stop. No further collection, replacement cities or relaxed
criteria were used to obtain a pass. Zero completed candidates means the ten-experiment program review was
not reached; no filler experiments were added. Candidate001 remains an unexecuted proposal.

compliance is a separate quality concern from estimator error.

Review preparation initially omitted disputes from invalid records. A separate, documented execution repair

within the reviewed set and did not create a new primary reference.

The Bled menu accounting start preceded actual dispatch while the coordinator audited the serializer. This
queue delay must not be attributed to model latency. The three missing artifacts and formatting defects limit
what can be concluded about acquisition feasibility beyond this executed design. The failed gate is not proof
that independent accuracy measurement is impossible.

## Recommendation and possible future design

Keep v1.1 unchanged. The justified verdict is **insufficient evidence for a comparative improvement**, rather
than a measured failure of a candidate. The earlier sparse offer comparison remains descriptive; it cannot
fill missing references or justify a universal adjustment. There is no finalist and no confirmation result.

If research is restarted separately, first design and test a smaller, explicit reference-acquisition contract:
complete machine schemas, narrow observations, source-payable charge basis, interruption-safe artifact
delivery and realistic source-access limits. Decide the measurable target prospectively: named-offer
reproduction, city-market estimates, a defined daily basket or actual spending. Private receipts/spending
would require separate authorization. Any changed source rules or eligibility need a fresh designer-authored
revision and preserved budgets; they cannot retroactively rescue this run. These lessons are not authorization
to dispatch more work now.
